Convert audio files and extract the first audio track from videos on your device. Video Converter works offline without accounts or telemetry.

Pick files, share them from another app, or scan a folder and its subfolders. Export MP3, AAC, ALAC, Opus, Vorbis, FLAC, or WAV. Save to Downloads/VideoConverter or choose a destination folder. You can keep the input folder structure when exporting.

Save conversion presets, trim leading and trailing silence, and normalize loudness. Run files in parallel, follow progress in a notification, and retry unfinished files after an interruption while keeping completed exports.

Requires Android 10 or newer and an arm64-v8a or x86_64 device.
